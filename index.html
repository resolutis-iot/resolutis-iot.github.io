About the Lab

Built for practitioners, not pitch decks.

The Manchester IoT Lab is a hands-on technical resource for hardware startups and engineering teams building edge AI and IoT products. We provide access to production silicon, embedded engineering depth, and a community of practitioners in the North of England.

Operated by Resolutis, the lab brings 25 years of embedded systems experience from Nokia, Google, JLR, Intel, and NXP to bear on the practical problems startups face when moving from development kit to production hardware.

The Manchester, Leeds and Sheffield triangle is underserved for this kind of support. The lab changes that.

NXP Partnership

NXP’s beachhead in Northern England.

The Manchester IoT Lab is positioned as NXP’s strategic presence in the Northern England embedded and edge AI ecosystem. Resolutis provides the operational backbone and engineering credibility. NXP gets a developer pipeline, brand presence, and a real-world proving ground for their silicon.

01
NXP Validated Lab

A formal NXP designation carried by the lab when enabling startups on NXP silicon — i.MX 9x, NTAG 424 DNA, SE050.

02
Startup Fast-Track

Lab members building on NXP silicon get a direct referral into NXP’s startup partnering programme via Resolutis as named sponsor.

03
Build on NXP Workshop Series

Co-branded quarterly events counting toward NXP developer community activity in the North of England.

NVIDIA Connect Partner

NVIDIA Jetson expertise, formally recognised.

Resolutis holds NVIDIA Connect membership, giving the lab and its startups direct access to NVIDIA's partner ecosystem, technical resources, and Jetson hardware programme. Edge AI inference on Jetson Orin is a core lab capability — not just a dev kit on a shelf.

01
Jetson Hardware in the Lab

Jetson AGX Orin, Orin NX, and Orin Nano available for prototyping sprints and inference bring-up. Access production-grade hardware before committing to your bill of materials.

02
TensorRT and DeepStream Deployment

Lab engineering support for TensorRT optimisation, INT8 quantisation, and DeepStream pipeline bring-up. Get your model from notebook to production Jetson hardware with the latency and power budget your product actually requires.

03
EdgeOps Observability

The lab runs EdgeOps — Resolutis’s in-house inference observability tool — on Jetson hardware. Latency tracking, model drift detection, and throughput dashboards. Datadog for edge inference at the SME price point.
