The Problem
01

Businesses have the vision for intelligent hardware. They can't find anyone who can actually build it.

AI engineers have never touched real hardware. Embedded engineers have never deployed a model. The people who can bridge both — from Linux kernel to production inference — are genuinely scarce. Most are locked inside large organisations. The businesses that need them most can't reach them.

That's the gap Manchester Edge AI Lab exists to close.

Mission

To build world-class embedded systems and edge AI capability in the North — developing local talent, serving Northern businesses, and positioning the region at the forefront of intelligent hardware.

What We Do

From silicon selection
to production.

07 capabilities
01

Edge AI Deployment

TensorRT, eIQ, ONNX Runtime. Get your trained model running on real hardware at the latency and power budget your product actually requires.

02

AI Observability

EdgeOps — real-time inference health monitoring for Jetson and i.MX deployments. Know what your edge AI is doing in production.

03

Hardware Prototyping

Bring-up on NXP i.MX 9x, NVIDIA Jetson and Microscontrollers (Arduino - ESP32). BSP customisation, Linux kernel drivers, sensor integration. No dev kit theatre.

04

NFC Authentication

NTAG 424 DNA cryptographic verification. Secure product identity for supply chain, luxury goods, and access control.

05

Edge-to-ERP Pipelines

Edge or on-prem devices to ERP dashboard. Rust-based data pipelines from device to cloud via Conduo.

06

Edge to Cloud

LTE-M, NB-IoT, LoRaWAN. From cellular tracker into AWS, GCP and Azure Cloud.

07

Training & Lab Access

Workshops, lab days, and open sessions. Production silicon access for Northern hardware startups and university spin-outs.
