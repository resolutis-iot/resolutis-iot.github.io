01

Manufacturing & Industry

Edge intelligence on the factory floor — reducing downtime, improving quality, cutting energy costs.

Predictive Maintenance

Machine Failure Detection Before It Happens

Vibration and thermal sensors on factory equipment feed edge AI models running on NVIDIA Jetson Orin. The system detects anomalies in motor behaviour, bearing wear, and thermal patterns — alerting engineers before failure occurs. All inference runs locally. No raw sensor data leaves the facility.

NVIDIA Jetson Orin TensorRT Vibration Sensors EdgeOps LPWAN
Quality Inspection

Real-Time Defect Detection

Computer vision running locally on Jetson Orin identifies surface defects on a production line in real time. No cloud round-trip. No latency. No raw image data leaving the facility. Confidence scores and audit logs via EdgeOps.

YOLOv8 DeepStream On-Device Only EdgeOps
Energy Monitoring

Smart Factory Power Intelligence

Distributed edge nodes tracking machine power consumption across a factory floor. Local anomaly detection surfaces energy waste and identifies inefficient equipment — piped through Conduo to existing ERP without infrastructure overhaul.

NXP i.MX 9x BLE Mesh Conduo ERP Integration

Supply Chain & Logistics

Authentication, tracking, and condition monitoring across the full product journey.

Product Authentication

NFC-Authenticated Product Passports

Physical goods tagged with NTAG 424 DNA chips. Each tap generates a cryptographically unique response — impossible to clone, verifiable at every point in the supply chain from production to end consumer with a standard smartphone.

NTAG 424 DNA AWS KMS NIST SP 800-108 PN532 SUN/SDM
Cold Chain

Perishable Goods Monitoring

Edge nodes tracking temperature and humidity on perishable goods through the full logistics chain. Local alerting when thresholds breach — no cloud dependency for critical alerts. Data piped to ERP via Conduo.

Asset Tracking

Connected Asset Visibility

LPWAN-connected tags on pallets, tools, and vehicles across Northern distribution networks. Real-time location, condition monitoring, and geofence alerting — feeding directly into warehouse management systems.

LoRaWAN NXP i.MX 9x Conduo Pipeline AWS IoT
